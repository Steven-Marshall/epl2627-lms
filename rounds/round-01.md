## Commentary

**Hull 2-0 Manchester United** was the shock of the weekend and the whole story of our
pool. Total underdogs, first day back up, and they do to United exactly what Grimsby
did in the cup. The **five who took United — the *disciplined* pick — all dropped a
life**, while the twelve-strong Arsenal herd cruised through 3-0.

The irony writes itself: the crowd that "over-harvested" Arsenal looks smart, and the
sharp minority who saved their ace took the hit. **But being right about strategy is a
long game.** Those five still hold Arsenal, City and every other weapon; the Arsenal
twelve are a life richer but have already spent their best card. The harvest/save
trade hasn't paid — it may still.

A word on the model: it gave United 70%, and a 30% event landed (Hull were a ~10% home
shot). That's variance, not a miss — one game is never a reason to re-rate a team, so
the ratings stand. The market will keep pricing each round.

And **Malley** is the week's quiet winner: survived on a 44% maverick punt *and* stayed
differentiated. The World Cup hero can't help himself — and once again it came off.
