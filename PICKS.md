# EPL 2026/27 LMS — the running story

A Last-Man-Standing pool over the 2026/27 Premier League. One pick a round; it must
**win** (a draw or loss costs a life); **3 lives**; **no repick**. ~10–20 mates,
split pot. Modelled round by round by "Paul the Octopus" 🐙 — who, for the record,
knows the numbers a lot better than he knows the football.

---

## Round 1 (Aug 21–24)

**Steven's pick: Manchester United @ Hull City (70%).**

The model's three best picks were Arsenal v Coventry (**83%**), Man Utd @ Hull
(**70%**) and Man City v Bournemouth (**66%**). Arsenal and City are being *saved* —
City for a monster home game against Hull in Round 16 (88%), Arsenal for its own Hull
game in Round 10 (91%). Man Utd was the **cheapest strong team to spend**: its best
upcoming fixture is barely better than this one, so playing it now banks a solid 70%
while keeping both aces in reserve. Textbook harvest-and-save.

**The personal bit.** Steven's a **Grimsby Town** fan. Last season the Mariners
turned Manchester United over in the cup — comfortably one of United's lowest moments
of the year. So sending United down the road to do a number on **Hull** — Grimsby's
neighbours — is a double pleasure: the club that humbled you doing your dirty work
against the one on your doorstep. 🐟
